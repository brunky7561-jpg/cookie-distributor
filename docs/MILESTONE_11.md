# Milestone 11: See it, try it, make it look good

## Why this exists
Playtesting said "nothing changed". The place file did contain every milestone, but almost all new features sit behind levels, cash or first orders, so a fresh save looked identical to Milestone 1. This pass makes the changes visible straight away and restyles the look.

## Try everything immediately (Studio only)
Press Play, then tap **Dev: unlock everything** (bottom-left, below the reset button). You jump to level 8 with $25,000, every recipe, all four properties, both vehicles, all equipment and a stocked pantry. Open the phone (P): all 11 apps are unlocked. Your owned buildings appear on their lots.

## What you will now see
- **Welcome card** on every join, and a glowing **COOKIE EMPIRE arch** by the spawn.
- **Golden-hour lighting** with stars, a slow day/night cycle (about 14 minutes), warm glow from every street lamp at night, depth of field, and sparkles on the cookie monument and fountain.
- **Your buildings**: the Food Truck, Garage and Corner Shop you buy appear on their lots (client-side, only you see yours) with your bakery name on a lit sign.
- **Restyled UI**: gradient cards and buttons, rounder corners, an app-icon phone home screen with emoji icons, NEW badges, a phone bezel, and emoji on the HUD.

## Where to find things on the map
- Food Truck lot: (-14, -86). Garage lot: (-75, -110). Corner Shop lot: (28, -68). Delivery mats are 10 to 14 studs from each lot.
